Poultry & Livestock Farming in Malawi

Farming can be profitable if done smartly. Here’s how to start with chickens and sheep.

1. Start Small

Begin with a few chickens or sheep and learn the basics.

2. Feed & Care

Proper feeding, clean water, and shelter are essential.

3. Selling & Market

Build relationships with local buyers and butcheries to sell your livestock at good prices.

4. Extra Tips

  • Keep records of expenses and sales.
  • Vaccinate animals on time.
  • Learn from experienced farmers.
